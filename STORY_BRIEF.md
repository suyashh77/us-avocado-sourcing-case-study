# Story brief and storyboard

**Title:** The avocado chain has one dominant lane.

**Thesis:** Mexico remains the dominant year-round U.S. origin. Peru and Colombia offer meaningful seasonal diversification, but existing alternative flows and current trade commitments do not support a complete Mexico replacement in the 2024 stress test.

**Audience:** Retail buyers, supply-chain planners, investors, and curious readers.

**Why it matters:** A list of avocado-producing countries can give a false impression of resilience. The operational question is whether available, compatible fruit can be delivered into the U.S. market at the right time and cost.

**Strongest exhibit:** The executive read and origin comparison connect 2025 U.S. import lanes to the 2024 Mexico-zero calculation: 1.066 million tonnes removed, 750,409 tonnes in the extreme current-supplier diversion pool, and a 315,949-tonne residual gap.

**Major caveat:** Redirectable supply is an assumption applied to already committed exports. The cost band is arithmetic, not a calibrated price forecast.

|---|---|---|---|
